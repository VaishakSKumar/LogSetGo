LogSetGo Privacy Policy

Track. Rest. Progress. · Effective 21 September 2026

The short version: LogSetGo does not collect, send, sell or share your data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Everything you log stays on your own device.

What LogSetGo stores

To work, the app saves the information you enter, on your device only:

  • Workouts: exercises, sets, weights, reps, warm-up flags, RPE and notes
  • Attendance marks (Present, Absent, Holiday)
  • Body weight entries and your height
  • Routines, goals, rest-timer presets and your settings

This is health and fitness information. It is stored in your phone's app storage, or in your browser's local storage if you use the web version. It is not uploaded anywhere.

What we do not do

  • We do not create accounts or ask for your name, email, phone number or location.
  • We do not use advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking services.
  • We do not transfer your data to us or to any third party.

Notifications

If you turn on rest-timer alerts or a daily reminder in Settings, the app asks your phone for permission and schedules notifications locally on the device. No notification service or server is involved. You can turn them off at any time in Settings or in your phone's system settings.

Backups and exports

You can export a backup or CSV from Settings. That file is created on your device and goes only where you choose to save or share it. Keep backups safe, since they contain your health and fitness data.

Error logs

If the app hits an error, a short technical note is kept on your device (the last 20). Nothing is sent automatically. If you choose "Share log" in Settings, you decide who receives it.

The web version

The web version is a static website served by GitHub Pages. GitHub, as the host, may record standard web-server request information (such as your IP address) under its own privacy statement. LogSetGo itself sets no cookies and loads no third-party scripts. Once installed to your home screen, the app works offline.

Deleting your data

Because everything is on your device, you are in control: uninstall the app, or clear the site's data in your browser, and it is gone. We cannot recover it, so export a backup first if you want to keep it.

Children

LogSetGo is a general fitness tracker and is not directed at children under 13. It collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.
